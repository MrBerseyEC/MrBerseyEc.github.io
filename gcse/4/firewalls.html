A firewall is a network security system that acts as a barrier between an organisation's internal network and the internet. It inspects the data traffic going in and out, and uses a set of rules to decide what to let through.

Set the rules

You are in charge of this company's firewall. Switch on the rules you want, then run the traffic through it. The aim is to stop every threat without getting in the way of people doing their jobs.

The organisation that owns a firewall customises the rules to suit its own circumstances. Rules that are too loose let threats through. Rules that are too strict stop the network being useful. Firewalls also flag up suspicious activity taking place on the internal network.

Hardware and software firewalls

A hardware firewall is a separate device between the network and the internet. A software firewall is a program installed on one computer. Take the company laptop out of the office and see what is protecting each device.
