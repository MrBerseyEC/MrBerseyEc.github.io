Wi-Fi isn't the only way devices talk without wires. These four technologies are all short range, and you probably used most of them today.

Bluetooth

Links two nearby devices directly, with no router in between. Turn Bluetooth on, pair a device, then walk away from it.

🎧 Wireless earbuds⌚ Smartwatches🚗 Hands-free calls in cars🎮 Game controllers⌨️ Keyboards and mice🔊 Portable speakers

Zigbee

A low-power network for smart home devices. Each mains-powered device passes messages on to the next, so a command can hop right across the house. Click a device to control it, or switch some off at the wall and watch the messages find another way round.

💡 Smart bulbs🔌 Smart plugs🔥 Smart heating🚪 Door and motion sensors⚡ UK smart meters🏢 Office lighting systems

Click the 🚪 front door sensor, or any light.

Radio-frequency identification (RFID)

A reader sends out radio waves. Any tag inside its range wakes up and replies with its unique ID. No battery, no scanning by hand, and it doesn't need to be facing the reader.

📦 Warehouse stock📚 Library self-service🏃 Race timing chips🐶 Pet microchips🛍️ Shop security tags🧳 Airport baggage tags⛷️ Ski lift passes

Near field communication (NFC)

Only works when the two devices are almost touching. Drag the card towards the reader (or press Tap) and watch what happens at about 4 cm.

💳 Contactless payment📱 Phone and watch wallets🚇 Travel cards at ticket barriers🏨 Hotel room keys🏷️ Smart posters and tags🪪 Passports and school ID cards
