Ripple-Carry Adder

Chaining full adders

Bit 0 has no carry coming in, so a half adder is enough there. Every other column uses a full adder, which adds its A bit, its B bit and the carry from the column to its right. Each carry-out feeds the next adder to the left. Click any input bit to flip it, then press Ripple to watch the carry travel.

Why it's called "ripple carry"

Column i can't finish until it knows its carry-in, which comes from column i−1. So the carry ripples one adder at a time from right to left. Each full adder adds about two gate delays to the carry path, so an n-bit ripple adder needs roughly 2n gate delays in the worst case.
