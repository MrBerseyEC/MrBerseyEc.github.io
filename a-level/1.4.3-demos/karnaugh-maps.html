Click a cell to switch it between 0 and 1. The map finds the largest groups of 1s and turns each group into one term of the simplest expression.

Notation
Inputs

    Grouping rules

    • Groups contain only 1s.
    • Group sizes are 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16, in rectangles.
    • Make each group as large as possible.
    • Use as few groups as possible.
    • Groups may overlap and may wrap around the edges.
    • A variable that changes inside a group drops out of its term.
