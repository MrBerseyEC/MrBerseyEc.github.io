Boolean simplification

Every rule is proved by a truth table: if both sides give the same output for every input, they are equivalent and one can replace the other.

Notation

The rules

Pick a rule, flip the inputs, and watch both sides agree. Most rules come in pairs: swap AND with OR and 0 with 1 to get the other form.

Worked examples

Reveal one step at a time. Each new step adds a column to the truth table, and the columns never change, so each step is equivalent to the start.

    Type two expressions to see if they are equivalent. Use A to D, 0, 1 and brackets, with ! or NOT, & or AND, | or OR. A' and AB also work.
